The challenge

The owner of a football sponsorship activation agency needed to stay on top of every new brand and club partnership announcement across the Premier League, Championship, League One, League Two, and Europe's top five leagues. Every single day.

Announcements are scattered across sports publications, club press releases, Google News, and niche lower-league outlets
Manually tracking all of this would take hours every morning and was essentially impossible to do comprehensively
The owner had higher-leverage work to focus on and couldn't afford to spend the morning reading sports news
Lower league clubs rarely appear in mainstream sports publications, making them especially hard to track manually

Our approach

A two-workflow n8n automation system that scrapes multiple news sources daily, filters for relevant announcements using AI, and delivers a branded email digest to the owner's inbox at 8am every morning.

01Main pipeline scrapes RSS and Google News, filters with AI, sends at 8am
02Lower-league scraper runs four times a day to catch what the main pass misses

What we built

01

Triggers at 8am

Main pipeline

Pulls from RSS feeds covering major sports-business publications, and queries Google News with targeted search strings to surface announcements not covered by specialist outlets. Filters all articles from the previous 24 hours, then passes the merged data to OpenAI, which applies chain-of-thought reasoning to each item, identifying the brand, the club, and the commercial action. Only items that explicitly announce or update a qualifying commercial partnership are kept, then formatted into a branded HTML email and sent via Gmail as The Football Partnership Brief.

02

Runs 6am, 12pm, 6pm, midnight

Lower league scraper

Scrapes a dedicated news aggregator four times daily, since it updates frequently enough that running once a day would miss earlier stories. Extracts article content and passes it through the same AI filtering logic as the main workflow. Results are aggregated and fed into the main workflow at 8am, where they're merged with the RSS data before the final email is generated.

The client has specifically noted the system has surfaced partnership news they'd have otherwise missed entirely. Announcements that would never have crossed their radar without it.
