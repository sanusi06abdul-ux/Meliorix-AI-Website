Privacy notice

Who we are: Meliorix AI (abdul@meliorixai.com) is responsible for the information you share here.

When you see your results: your answers (but no name, email or contact details) are sent to us anonymously. We use them to work out your results, write a short personalised summary using an AI service (Anthropic's Claude), and improve this tool. Please don't type names, client details or anything confidential into the free-text box.

If you ask for your plan by email: we also store your email address and, if you give them, your first name and company. We use your email only to:

  • send the plan you asked for,
  • arrange a free call, only if you tick that box,
  • send occasional tips, only if you tick that separate box. Asking for your plan doesn't sign you up.

Storage: answers are kept in a private, access-restricted Google Sheet and processed using n8n, Anthropic and our email provider (Resend). We delete them after 12 months unless we're working together. To see, correct or delete your data, email abdul@meliorixai.com.
