The challenge

This client ran a membership programme across five tiers with no automation in place. Every renewal meant a member manually re-entering payment details, and every new ticket had to be issued by hand. Every purchase, renewal, or cancellation required manual tracking and communication. With members spread across five different tiers, keeping records accurate and sending the right communication at the right time had become unmanageable.

New membership tickets had to be issued manually on every renewal
Members had to manually re-enter payment details for a subscription to renew
No centralised record of who had purchased, activated, or cancelled their membership
No automated reminders for members who purchased but never activated
No automated renewal reminders before a member's billing date
Cancellations had to be tracked manually across multiple systems

Our approach

We built a fully automated end-to-end membership management system across Ticket Tailor, Pabbly Connect, Pabbly Subscriptions, and Google Sheets, covering every stage of the member lifecycle without any manual input required.

The system centres on a two-tab Google Sheet acting as the membership database. The All Contacts tab logs every purchase with the member's name, email, tier, order date, activation status, and reminder status. The Active Members tab tracks every activated member with their tier, price, renewal date, days until renewal, and active or cancelled status. Both tabs update automatically as members move through the lifecycle.

01

New purchase

Triggered instantly when a new order is placed in Ticket Tailor. Filters for membership orders only, timestamps the purchase, logs the member to the All Contacts tab, and routes them to the correct tier-specific onboarding email.

02

Subscription activated

Triggered when a member completes activation in Pabbly Subscriptions. Updates their record, adds them to the Active Members tab with their renewal date, and sends a tier-specific confirmation email.

03

Reminder to activate

Runs daily. Checks for members who purchased a day ago and haven't yet activated, and sends a reminder with their checkout link, matched to the right tier.

04

Subscription about to renew

Runs daily. Checks for members whose renewal date is three days away and who haven't yet received a reminder, sends one, and marks it sent to prevent duplicates.

05

Subscription renewed

Triggered instantly when a recurring payment succeeds. Updates the renewal date and automatically issues a new membership ticket via the Ticket Tailor API, no one has to lift a finger.

06

Membership cancelled

Triggered instantly when a subscription is cancelled. Updates the member's status to cancelled in both tracking tabs. Pabbly Subscriptions handles the cancellation confirmation email automatically.

The client received full documentation, including a written SOP, workflow diagrams for all six automations, and video walkthroughs covering how the system works and how to update pricing independently.
