FAQ

Questions owners ask before booking a call.

Do I need to know what to automate?

No. Bring the task that eats up your team's time. On the call we walk through it together and tell you honestly whether automation is the right fix. If you'd like a head start, the free calculator shows what a task is costing you in about 3 minutes.

What happens on the free call?

It's 30 minutes with no obligation. We look at one process you'd like to fix, ask how it runs today, and give you a straight answer on whether it's worth automating and what that would involve.

What kind of businesses do you work with?

Owner-led service businesses, such as agencies, letting agents, home services and professional firms. If your team loses hours to copying, chasing or re-checking the same things, we can probably help.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the process, so we scope it with you before anything starts. A pilot has a fixed scope, so you know what you're getting before you commit.

How long does it take?

A pilot usually takes 2 to 4 weeks, from first call to an automation running in your business.

Do I have to commit long term?

No. Most people start with a pilot on one process and get a clear go or no-go at the end. Ongoing partner work runs month to month.

Will we have to change our tools?

No. We build around the tools you already use, such as your inbox, CRM, spreadsheets and calendar. Nothing needs to be migrated.

Do you use AI for everything?

No. We use AI where it genuinely adds something and plain automation where it doesn't. One of our client builds runs a whole membership programme with no AI at all.

Will my team need to learn anything new?

Very little. You get SOPs, diagrams and a video walkthrough, so you and your team know exactly how it works.

What happens after it goes live?

We stay close while it settles in. As your business changes, we tune the automation to match, then move on to the next process holding you back.

What if our process changes later?

Tell us. Everything is documented, so changes are straightforward, and partner clients get priority tuning when new needs come up.

Got a bottleneck? Let's fix it.

Whether you've already got a clear idea or you've just noticed something's slow, tell us about it. No pressure, no jargon, just an honest read on whether automation is the right fix.
